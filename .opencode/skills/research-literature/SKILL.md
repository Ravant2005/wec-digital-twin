# Literature Synthesis Protocol

When evaluating or comparing literature:
1. Always base claims on actual evidence found in primary literature.
2. If comparing approaches, use an ablation ladder and distinguish between simulated vs empirical results.
3. Keep track of specific constraints (e.g., Goa-region Arabian Sea conditions) and do not generalize arbitrarily.
4. Report the uncertainty or limitations of the literature, not just the positive claims.
