CONCLUSIONS:
  1. Within production range (omega <= 1.4 rad/s):
     Hull-only and hull+lid differ by 5-47% in A and 19-67% in B.
     This is NOT just irregular-frequency suppression; the lid
     fundamentally changes the BEM formulation at all frequencies.
  2. Above omega_irr (>= 2.09 rad/s):
     Hull-only shows unphysical behavior (negative A, erratic B).
     Hull+lid suppresses the artifact but introduces its own errors.
  3. Decision: retain hull-only for production (omega <= 1.4 rad/s).
     A lid is recommended only if the frequency range must exceed 1.4 rad/s.
     The choice of lid vs hull-only requires validation against
     an independent reference (e.g. analytical or published BEM results).
