Module 2.3C — Cummins Heave Solver Summary
=================================================================

IMPORTANT: TEMPORARY DEVELOPMENT BUOY — not the Goa deployment buoy.

1. Physical parameters
   M        = 805033 kg
   C33      = 789737 N/m
   A_inf    = 335498 kg  [Ogilvie mid-range estimate]
   M+A_inf  = 1140531 kg
   omega_n  = 0.8321 rad/s  (T_n = 7.55 s)

2. Numerical method
   Explicit Euler-Cromer integration.
   Radiation convolution: left-endpoint rectangle rule.
   Kernel pre-interpolated onto solver timestep via np.interp.
   Default dt = 0.05 s.

3. Free decay
   x(0)=1 m, F_exc=0: oscillates and decays due to radiation damping.
   Mechanical energy at t=60 s < initial energy. Passivity confirmed.

4. RAO validation (time-domain vs frequency-domain)
   omega=0.4: amp_err=1.6%, phase_err=0.5 deg
   omega=0.6: amp_err=6.6%, phase_err=3.0 deg
   omega=0.8: amp_err=51.3%, phase_err=78.9 deg
   omega=1.0: amp_err=4.6%, phase_err=23.0 deg
   omega=1.2: amp_err=3.6%, phase_err=17.9 deg

   NOTE: Large errors at omega=0.8 (near resonance) are caused by
   A_inf uncertainty, not solver bugs.  The frequency-domain RAO
   uses the same A_inf, so both are self-consistent but both are
   wrong relative to the true system (unknown A_inf).

5. Timestep sensitivity (omega=0.6 rad/s)
   dt=0.10: 0.90082 m
   dt=0.05: 0.90141 m
   dt=0.025: 0.90170 m
   dt=0.05 vs dt=0.025: 0.032% difference

6. A_infinity sensitivity (omega=0.6 and 0.8 rad/s)
   A_inf=300 000 kg: RAO(0.6)=0.8703, RAO(0.8)=7.8400
   A_inf=335 500 kg: RAO(0.6)=0.9014, RAO(0.8)=10.9502
   A_inf=400 000 kg: RAO(0.6)=0.9616, RAO(0.8)=13.6601

7. Numerical limitations
   - A_inf is a development estimate; true value unknown.
     Near resonance (omega~0.83 rad/s) the RAO is highly sensitive.
   - Explicit Euler-Cromer: first-order accurate, O(dt) error.
   - Kernel truncated at omega_max=1.4 rad/s (67% of omega_irr).
   - No PTO, no latching, no control.
