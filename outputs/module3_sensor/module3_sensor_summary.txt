SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS
  1. Synthetic sensor only — noise parameters are development assumptions.
  2. No actual camera image processing is implemented.
  3. No wave segmentation or tracking is implemented.
  4. No environmental occlusion model (sea-spray, glare, rain, fog).
  5. No spatial camera geometry is modelled.
  6. No uncertainty calibration against real buoy-camera data.
  7. Noise is i.i.d. Gaussian; real camera errors are correlated.
  8. No high-frequency synthetic observations (ERA5 is hourly).
